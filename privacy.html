Major Notes

Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Major Notes does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Your notes stay on your device

Everything you write in Major Notes, including your notes, your peg list, and your settings, is stored only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking

The app has no sign-in, contains no advertising or analytics code, does not track you across apps or websites, and makes no network connections.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your app data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. We have no access to it.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.
