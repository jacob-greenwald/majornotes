Support

Questions, bug reports, or ideas? Contact us (opens a support request; a free GitHub account is needed). If something isn't working, include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I see the words for a number?

Open a note and tap on any number. A brain button appears above the keyboard. Tap it to see memory words, swap any word for another, and add them to your note.

How does the Major system work?

Each digit is a consonant sound: 0 = s/z, 1 = t/d, 2 = n, 3 = m, 4 = r, 5 = l, 6 = j/sh/ch, 7 = k/hard g, 8 = f/v, 9 = p/b. Vowels and w, h, y don't count, so 14 can be "tire" and 32 can be "moon". Tap the brain icon on the main screen for the full guide.

Can I use my own words?

Yes. Open the guide from the brain icon on the main screen, then My Peg List. Enter your own word for any number from 0–9 and 00–99. Your words are always suggested first, and the app shows a warning if a word doesn't match its number.
