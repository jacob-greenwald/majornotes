Major Notes

Remember any number with words

Major Notes is a notes app for iPhone built around the Major system, the classic memory technique that turns digits into consonant sounds and numbers into words you can picture.

  • Tap any number in a note to see memory words for it
  • Swap any word for an alternative with one tap
  • Build your own peg list for 0–9 and 00–99
  • A built-in calculator that shows memory words for every result

No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
